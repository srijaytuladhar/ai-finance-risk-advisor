# Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
The purpose of this Investment Policy Statement (IPS) is to establish clear guidelines, risk parameters, and asset allocation targets for the Fintech Risk Advisor model portfolio. The primary objective is to achieve long-term capital appreciation with moderate volatility, prudent risk diversification across major economic sectors, and rigorous downside capital protection.

## 2. Risk Tolerance and Investment Profile
The investor maintains a Moderate-Growth risk profile with an investment horizon exceeding five years. The portfolio strategy is designed to balance equity growth with defensive capital preservation. Key quantitative risk boundaries established for this mandate include:
- Maximum Acceptable 1-Year Drawdown: -20.0%
- Target Annualized Volatility: Between 12.0% and 18.0%
- Expected Sharpe Ratio: Greater than 1.0 under standard economic conditions
- Maximum 1-Day Value at Risk (VaR) at 95% Confidence: 2.50% of total portfolio equity
- Target Portfolio Beta relative to S&P 500 (SPY): 0.85 to 1.15

## 3. Asset Allocation and Target Weights
To achieve optimal risk-adjusted returns without excessive exposure to any single company or sector, assets must be allocated within predefined asset classes and target weight bands:

- Large-Cap Technology (e.g., AAPL, MSFT): Target 25.0% (Allowable Band: 20.0% - 30.0%)
- Financial Services (e.g., JPM): Target 15.0% (Allowable Band: 10.0% - 20.0%)
- Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals (e.g., JNJ): Target 15.0% (Allowable Band: 10.0% - 20.0%)
- Energy & Natural Resources (e.g., XOM): Target 10.0% (Allowable Band: 5.0% - 15.0%)
- Broad Market Index / ETF (e.g., SPY): Target 20.0% (Allowable Band: 15.0% - 25.0%)
- Cash & Liquidity Reserves: Target 15.0% (Allowable Band: 10.0% - 20.0%)

No individual stock holding outside of broad-market index ETFs shall exceed 20.0% of the aggregate portfolio market value at the time of quarterly review.

## 4. Rebalancing Guidelines and Trigger Rules
Disciplined rebalancing prevents drift and mitigates sector concentration risk. The portfolio adheres to a hybrid calendar-and-band rebalancing methodology:

1. **Tolerance Band Trigger**: If any individual asset class or sector weighting drifts by more than 5.0% absolute deviation from its stated target allocation (for example, if Technology exceeds 30.0% or drops below 20.0%), a rebalancing evaluation is automatically triggered.
2. **Semi-Annual Calendar Review**: Comprehensive rebalancing assessments are conducted at mid-year (June 30) and calendar year-end (December 31) regardless of drift margins.
3. **Execution Protocol**: Rebalancing transactions should prioritize tax efficiency and minimize transaction costs. Where possible, new cash contributions, dividend accruals, or interest payments should be directed toward under-weighted assets to restore target balance before liquidating profitable positions.
4. **Emergency Stop-Loss & De-risking**: If a portfolio's rolling 30-day drawdown approaches -15.0%, the advisor is mandated to review underlying fundamentals and temporarily adjust liquidity reserves up to 25.0% until market volatility stabilizes.
